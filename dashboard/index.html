Recommendations

  1. Peak-season playbookAdd carrier capacity and extend promised dates on long-haul lanes for Black Friday and Feb–Mar. Removes up to ~31% of late orders.
  2. Lane-aware delivery promiseSet the promised date for SP→RJ / BA / ES / CE from each lane's actual delivery history; A/B test the effect on conversion.
  3. Seller delivery SLA + watchlistMonthly scorecard for the 65 chronic-late sellers: coaching first, reduced search ranking if no improvement.
  4. Second-order CRM programmeTrigger offers between days 7 and 120 after the first purchase, led by high-repeat categories. +1 pp repeat ≈ R$120K / year; prove it with a holdout test.
  5. Win-back for dormant high-value customers16K customers (17%) who generated 41% of revenue and have not bought in ~10 months.
